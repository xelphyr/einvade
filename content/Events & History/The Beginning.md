## Summary
Before the existence of the Bubbles in the [[Overrealm]], there was a vast and endless ocean of [[Dephos]]. And in the [[Spiritrealm]], there was the unceasing brightness of [[Vastra]]. Some claim that these were the decaying remains of the Old Realms, hence explaining the diversity of fauna and flora between relatively closer Bubbles. 

There were few that remained during this darkness and lightness. Were they survivors? Were they born of the matter in the realm? All we know is that somehow, they tore open the space between the two realms, creating the first Bubbles.
